## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, executive-grade digital experience for modern enterprise SaaS reselling and software operations. It blends the authority of corporate software distribution with the forward-leaning energy of high-tech and web3-adjacent aesthetics. 

The visual personality is defined by:
- **Authority & Security:** Imparted through deep obsidian-navy base planes and disciplined geometric containment.
- **Power & Premium Exclusivity:** Delivered via dual-tier metallic gold accents, evocative of metallic gold emblem craft and championship-tier business tools.
- **High-Velocity Innovation:** Piercing neon cyan illumination, subtle laser wireframes, and luminous edge highlights that signal speed, real-time analytics, and automated operations.

The style operates as an elevated hybrid of **Refined Cyber-Glassmorphism** and **High-Contrast Dark Mode**. Interfaces leverage deep atmospheric layers, frosted smoke glass panels, hairline metallic gold borders, and glowing volumetric accents that guide attention directly toward conversion and system modules (Venda+, Gerente Virtual, TiAgenda).

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for deep nocturnal high-contrast readability, using a metallic warm highlight alongside an electric cybernetic beacon.

- **Primary (`#F5B700`):** Electric Auric Gold. Used for high-converting elements, highlighted price badges, critical CTAs, and metallic gradient headline strokes. Complemented by `#FFC837` (light gold highlight) and `#D4AF37` (antique metallic mid-tone).
- **Secondary (`#00D2FF`):** Cyber Cyan Neon. Used for telemetry indicators, live dashboard cues, secondary buttons, glowing card borders, and technological feature accents. Complemented by `#0070F3` (deep laser blue) for rich linear gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`):** Conversion WhatsApp Green. Dedicated exclusively to instant customer contact points, direct lead acquisition, and live support triggers.
- **Neutral (`#070B14`):** Deep Obsidian Navy. Serves as the canvas floor. Elevated surface steps utilize `#0A1124` (Card Base), `#101C38` (Elevated Glass Base), and `#1C2B52` (Interactive Hover Surface).
- **Text & Foreground:** High-luminance white (`#FFFFFF`) for bold headers, cool platinum (`#E2E8F0`) for primary body, and muted cyber slate (`#8E9CB2`) for secondary metadata and helper text.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear contrast between high-octane branding and legible operational content:

- **Headlines & Metric Figures (Montserrat):** Set in ExtraBold (800) and Black (900) weights with uppercase tracking (+0.04em) on major banners. High-tier titles leverage a linear gradient fill clip running from `#FFF4D0` through `#F5B700` to `#D4AF37`, imparting a polished metallic finish.
- **Body & Data Displays (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and clear at micro scales. Optimized for dense commercial lists, software features, and pricing structures with generous line heights to prevent visual crowding against dark backdrops.
- **Badges, Status Tags & Action Labels (Montserrat):** Compact, bold, and authoritative. Always capitalized with distinct letter-spacing (+0.06em) for instant identification across pricing tables and product tiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured upon a responsive 12-column fluid grid system that condenses to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Rhythm & Grid Alignment:** Modules and cards maintain uniform edge alignment. Content panels sit over a multi-layered coordinate plane with radial light blurs anchored behind hero focal points.
- **Density:** Medium-compact. Information density remains high to support software pricing breakdowns, module add-ons, and benefit matrices without demanding excessive vertical scrolling.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop (>= 1024px)`: 12-column grid, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Software suites and plan breakdowns layout across 3 primary product cards side-by-side or stacked product heroes with 3 internal utility tracks.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1023px)`: 6-column grid, 16px gutters, 24px canvas margins. Plan columns shift to 2-up configurations with sticky CTA docks.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: 4-column grid, 16px gutters, 16px canvas margins. Cards reflow into vertical execution stacks; pricing rows change to compact inline summary pills.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient glows and illuminated borders rather than traditional dark drop shadows:

- **Surface Layer 0 (Canvas):** Solid background in deep obsidian navy (`#070B14`) textured with subtle 45-degree cyber grid lines or diagonal neon streaks at 8% opacity.
- **Surface Layer 1 (Glass Cards):** Semi-transparent `#0A1124` at 85% opacity with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur. Outlined by a crisp `1px` gradient stroke transitioning from cyan neon (`#00D2FF` at 50%) to dark slate (`#1C2B52` at 20%).
- **Surface Layer 2 (Featured Tiers & Highlights):** Metallic Gold boundary layer. Outer glow defined by `0 0 24px rgba(245, 183, 0, 0.25), 0 0 2px rgba(255, 200, 55, 0.8)`. Surface fill uses `#101C38` with an interior top specular highlight.
- **Surface Layer 3 (Modals & Sticky Floating Bars):** Opaque dark glass (`#0A1124` at 95%) with a dual cyan/gold perimeter rim and `0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The geometric personality features controlled corner radii that balance sleek consumer glassmorphism with industrial tech angularity:

- Standard structural panels, pricing modules, and product viewports use `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `1rem` (`16px`) radius.
- Buttons, pricing chips, and high-impact badges incorporate chamfered tech corners or pill containers (`9999px`) to create clear tactile contrast against rectangular grids.
- Decorative accent frames and software screenshot containers utilize double-beveled hairline corners with glowing vertex brackets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion (Gold Radiant):** Solid gradient fill from `#FFC837` to `#F5B700`, text in rich dark navy (`#070B14`, 900 weight). Hover activates a localized neon gold pulse (`0 0 20px rgba(245, 183, 0, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Action (Cyber Cyan Ghost):** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#00D2FF`, text in `#00D2FF` or pure white. Hover fills with `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.12)`.
- **WhatsApp Action Button:** High-visibility `#25D366` green background with crisp white typography and a dynamic ping radar animation on the icon.

### Cards & Solution Modules
- **Software Suite Cards (Venda+, Gerente Virtual, TiAgenda):** Grounded on Surface Layer 1. The card header features a high-saturation software badge, gold-accented title, and a dual-column layout for core inclusions vs. optional modules.
- **Highlighted Plan Containers:** Bordered in solid metallic gold with an embedded top-center pill badge (`RECOMENDADO` or `SOLUÇÃO COMPLETA`) in gold fill with black text.

### Badges & Pricing Chips
- **Price Tag Chips:** High-contrast pill containers featuring a solid electric gold background (`#F5B700`) with bold black text (`R$ 159,00/mês`) or high-tech dark boxes with cyan border trims.
- **Tech Feature Badges:** Micro-pills with dark blue fill, neon cyan checkmark icons, and clean white body text.

### Lists & Feature Matrix
- Features employ high-luminance checkmark icons (`#00D2FF` or `#F5B700`) nested within circular glass backdrops.
- Module add-on rows (`WhatsApp`, `TEF POS`, `Multi-empresas`) feature clean alternating row dividers using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` with clear right-aligned price tags.

### Input Fields
- Dark navy surface (`#070B14`) with a `1px` border in `#1C2B52`. On focus, transitions to an electric cyan stroke (`#00D2FF`) with an ambient cyan glow (`0 0 8px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.3)`). Placeholder text in `#8E9CB2`.

### Interactive Components & Motion (.rocket-stage)
- **Localização:** Seção de CTA Final (`#cta-final`), coluna direita.
- **Estrutura:** `.rocket-stage` (HTML5 `<picture>` + `<canvas aria-hidden="true">`) com `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, `aria-label="Repetir decolagem do foguete"` e `data-loop`.
- **Assets:** `/assets/foguete.webp` (162 KB) com fallback para `/assets/foguete.png` (188 KB) em resolução nativa 1024x1024.
- **Propulsores (EMIT em coordenadas 1024px):**
  - Ejetor Esquerdo: `{ x: 245, y: 672, w: 30, c: '80,225,255' }` (Ciano Neon)
  - Ejetor Central: `{ x: 322, y: 752, w: 46, c: '255,200,90' }` (Dourado Metálico)
  - Ejetor Direito: `{ x: 440, y: 815, w: 32, c: '80,225,255' }` (Ciano Neon)
- **Duração das Fases (`T` em segundos):**
  - `ignite`: 1.3s (tremor e ignição progressiva de chama)
  - `climb`: 1.5s (aceleração diagonal ascendente com partículas e fumaça cibernética)
  - `away`: 0.5s (fora de tela)
  - `back`: 1.7s (reentrada suave desacelerando até repouso)
  - `idle`: pulso de chama contínuo e levitação suave. Repetição automática a cada 8s configurada via `data-loop`.
- **Controles de Desempenho e Acessibilidade:**
  - DPR limitado a 2; máximo de 600 partículas ativas no canvas.
  - Pausa de execução via `IntersectionObserver` (fora de visão) e `document.visibilitychange` (aba oculta).
  - Suporte estrito a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: cancelamento de loop, ocultação de canvas e repouso estático absoluto.
  - Botão de lupa do lightbox (`.showcase-lightbox-trigger`) isolado como irmão do palco, garantindo clique para ampliação sem conflito com o disparo da animação.
